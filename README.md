# Future Updates

We have an ambitious roadmap planned for the world of **Game**. Here are just a few of the major features on the horizon:

- **Religion System** – Found your own cult and guide its rise to prominence.
- **Underdark** – Explore an entirely new world map filled with underground adventures.
- **Multiple Races** – Encounter and play as a diverse range of races, each with unique traits.
- **Necromancy Updates** – Delve deeper into the dark arts with expanded necromantic abilities.
- **Demon Update** – Visit the underworld or demon realm and uncover its secrets.
- **Real-Time Strategy Elements Update** – Command your forces in dynamic, real-time encounters.
- **Story Mode** – Experience a narrative-driven campaign that reacts to your choices.
- **Map Painter** – Sketch and edit the overworld tile by tile to sculpt your ideal realm before embarking.

We are always listening to player suggestions—and there is much, much more to come!


The Complexity of the game comes from thousands of intersecting gameplay elements and mechanisms. Similar to Space Station-13 in how your actions can make everything fall apart. Let's say you pick an obscure class like an Alchemist. 

The way it would normally be played is you start with a small shop or if you picked a hold that doesn't have a proceedually generated alchamist shop for you to be assigned too start from your home. You would have ingrediants in your posession and be able to combine them through a crafting system NPCs get sick and family members would periodically come to you when to request your help in curing them. The more people you sucessfully save the more people come to you in general asking for general lotion to cure aching joints or mood stabalizers, the general health of the hold is increased. If your reputiation is high enough unemployed dwarves will come asking to apprentice for you and you can automate the job more and more, you can hire a dwarvern carpenter to expand your shop or even franchise it to have multiple ones across the hold if you pick one of the holds which doesn't have an alchamist guild as most don't you'll recieve a messanger from the alchamist guild in the nearest hold that has one offering to grant you a charter to open up one here. Farmers will approach you offering to begin growing certain ingrediants and gnome tinkerers will offer to build machines so you can begin producing your potions and ailments on an industrial scale if the hold has a closed policy that doesn't allow outsiders in you can lobby the Thane to change that using money or influence and merhants will begin freely coming and going, allowing you to create your own trade routes specifically for your goods, it can come to a point where you want to cut out the middleman of selling directly to merchants and hire your own trade caravans, sending them out to human cities and other dwarf holds, you would need to hire merchants, take out insurance policies on your wares with the commerce guild but the money would keep flowing and as your influence grew you could get more and more of a say in the politics of the hold. 

Or as an alchamist you can drug the food of the guards so they fall asleep, leaving the Thanes bedroom unguarded and allowing you to steal his blood so you can create a polymorph potion making you look like him, then simply put his body in a sack, bring it to some abandoned corner of the deeper floors of the hold, hire a locksmith to repair the lock on the door of that abandoned room and then hold him captive, periodically coming back to take more of his blood to make more potions so you can impersonate him indefeidnetly.

Or even simplier, find the water supply or an unguarded keg in the local brewery and fill it halusanegetics and see what happens.
